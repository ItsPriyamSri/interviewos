Company: Google
Role: Site Reliability Engineer (L4)

About the role

Site Reliability Engineers at Google keep large-scale production systems fast,
reliable, and operable. SREs blend software engineering with systems
engineering: they write automation to eliminate toil, design for reliability,
and respond to incidents across Google's fleet.

Responsibilities

- Design, build, and maintain automation for deployment, monitoring, and incident response.
- Own reliability targets (SLOs/SLIs/error budgets) for a service area.
- Troubleshoot production issues across networking, storage, compute, and Kubernetes-based platforms.
- Write code in Python, Go, or Java to improve observability and reduce manual operations.
- Participate in an on-call rotation and lead blameless postmortems.

Requirements

- Experience running and operating large-scale distributed systems.
- Programming experience in Python, Go, Java, or C++.
- Working knowledge of Linux internals, networking (TCP/IP, DNS, load balancing), and containers/Kubernetes.
- Experience with monitoring and alerting systems (e.g. Prometheus, Monarch-style metrics).
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.

Interview process overview

1. Recruiter screen (~30 min): background, motivation, logistics.
2. Online assessment or coding screen (for some candidates): 1-2 algorithmic problems.
3. Phone/virtual technical interview: data structures and algorithms.
4. Onsite loop (typically 4-5 rounds):
   - Coding round(s): algorithms and data structures in a chosen language.
   - Systems design / Distributed systems round: design at scale, trade-offs.
   - Non-abstract Large System Design (NALSD): capacity estimation, hardware sizing, failure analysis.
   - Googleyness & leadership: collaboration, ambiguity handling, values.
5. Team match: conversations with hiring teams after passing the loop.

What is evaluated

- Coding: correctness, complexity analysis, clean implementation.
- Systems thinking: failure modes, capacity planning, SLOs, trade-off reasoning.
- Troubleshooting: structured debugging under time pressure.
- Communication: clear reasoning aloud; asking clarifying questions.
